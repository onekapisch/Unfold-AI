Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Unfold AI operates entirely inside your browser. It reads rendered AI answers only to display a local summary, Answer Map, and progressive-reading controls. No text, prompts, summaries, identifiers, or usage data are transmitted to any server.

Local storage

Your settings and reveal state are saved in browser-local extension storage. Text enters extension-owned IndexedDB only when you explicitly select Save answer. Saved insights remain on this device until you export or delete them; Unfold has no remote backup.

Permissions

The extension requests the storage permission to save your preferences, and host permissions for the AI provider domains you enable (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Manus, Perplexity, DeepSeek). These permissions are used solely to inject the reading interface; no data is collected or transmitted.

Third parties

Unfold AI contains no analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party SDKs of any kind.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
