# Privacy Policy — Unfold AI

Last updated: September 4, 2026

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Unfold AI operates entirely inside your browser. It reads rendered AI answers only to display a local summary, Answer Map, and progressive-reading controls. No text, prompts, summaries, identifiers, or usage data are transmitted to any server.

## Local storage

Your settings and reveal state are saved in browser-local extension storage. Text enters extension-owned IndexedDB only when you explicitly select **Save answer**. Saved insights remain on this device until you export or delete them; Unfold has no remote backup.

## Permissions

The extension requests the `storage` permission to save your preferences, and host permissions for the AI provider domains you enable: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Manus, Perplexity, and DeepSeek. These permissions are used solely to inject the reading interface; no data is collected or transmitted.

## Third parties

Unfold AI contains no analytics, tracking pixels, advertising, or third-party SDKs.

## Contact

Questions or privacy concerns can be submitted through the [Unfold AI issue tracker](https://github.com/onekapisch/Unfold-AI/issues). Do not include private conversation text in a report.
