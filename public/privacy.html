Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2025

What we collect

Nothing. Unfold AI operates entirely inside your browser. It reads page content only to display a summary and progressive-reveal controls. No text, prompts, summaries, or usage data are ever transmitted to any server.

Local storage

Your settings (reading preset, word limits, enabled providers) are saved to chrome.storage.local on your own device. This data never leaves your browser and is not accessible to anyone other than you.

Permissions

The extension requests the storage permission to save your preferences, and host permissions for the AI provider domains you enable (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Manus, Perplexity, DeepSeek). These permissions are used solely to inject the reading interface; no data is collected or transmitted.

Third parties

Unfold AI contains no analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party SDKs of any kind.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
