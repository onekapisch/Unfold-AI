# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-09-04

## Product Overview

**One-liner:** Unfold AI privately summarizes long AI answers and turns them into a clickable Answer Map.
**What it does:** Unfold adds a genuine local summary, semantic navigation, progressive reading, and locally saved insights to supported AI conversations. The original answer remains intact and no account is required.
**Product category:** AI answer reader and conversation navigation browser extension.
**Product type:** Free Chrome and Firefox extension.
**Business model:** Free and open source in version 2.0.

## Target Audience

**Target users:** Researchers, developers, students, writers, analysts, ADHD readers, and other heavy AI-chat users.
**Primary use case:** Understand and revisit long AI answers without scrolling or rereading the entire response.
**Jobs to be done:**

- Find the main conclusion quickly.
- Jump to the exact section, source, action, or code block needed.
- Save one useful insight without building a cloud knowledge system.

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** Long AI answers are difficult to scan, navigate, and reuse.
**Why alternatives fall short:** Native chat interfaces preserve chronological output but provide weak response-level structure; broad toolboxes add complexity and often require more permissions, accounts, or cloud features.
**Emotional tension:** Users worry they missed the important sentence and waste time searching through a wall of text.

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct:** ChatTOC, Chat Navigator, Chat Outline, and Chat Collapse focus primarily on prompt or conversation navigation.
**Secondary:** AI Toolbox adds folders, search, prompts, and exports but is a broad organizer rather than a focused reading instrument.
**Indirect:** Manual browser find, copying into notes, or asking the AI to summarize its previous answer.

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:** Genuine local summary, response-level semantic map, deterministic action/source/code views, progressive reading, and private local saves.
**How we do it differently:** Unfold enhances the original answer in place instead of replacing the host AI or building a parallel cloud workspace.
**Why that is better:** The benefit appears immediately, conversation content stays local, and the original answer remains one click away.

## Objections

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| Will it read private chats? | It processes the rendered response locally and sends nothing to a server. |
| Will it change the answer? | The original response remains intact and can always be shown in full. |
| Does it require an account or API key? | No. Chrome can use its local built-in model; every browser has a local extractive fallback. |

**Anti-persona:** People who want a cloud team knowledge base, prompt automation suite, or autonomous browsing agent.

## Switching Dynamics

**Push:** Endless scrolling, losing the main point, and repeatedly copying whole answers.
**Pull:** Immediate structure, trustworthy local processing, and one-click access to the exact section.
**Habit:** Continuing to use browser find or asking the AI to summarize itself.
**Anxiety:** Granting page access or hiding important original content.

## Customer Language

**How they describe the problem:** “Long AI answers become a wall of text.”
**Words to use:** summarize locally, Answer Map, jump to a section, original answer, private, no account.
**Words to avoid:** AI output controller, magical, revolutionary, best, hallucination-free.

## Brand Voice

**Tone:** Calm, direct, precise.
**Style:** Plain verbs, truthful labels, minimal promotion.
**Personality:** Focused, trustworthy, intelligent, restrained.

## Proof Points

**Current metrics:** Six public installed users, zero ratings, and 34 install events in the latest 30-day export before version 2.0. Install events are not represented as unique or active users.
**Value themes:** Privacy, comprehension, navigation, and local ownership.

## Goals

**Business goal:** Validate retained demand for a focused AI answer reader.
**Conversion action:** Install Unfold AI and successfully unfold a long answer.
**Initial validation target:** Grow the public installed-user metric from 6 to at least 15 with positive net installs and five genuine reviews after release.
